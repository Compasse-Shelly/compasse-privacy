Compasse — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Compasse (“Compasse,” “we,” “us”) is a voice-friendly companion app for an older adult, set up and managed by a family member (the “caregiver”). This policy explains what we collect, why, who processes it, and your choices. Questions or requests: Aidan@revisesolutions.com.

Compasse is not a medical device, a safety-monitoring service, or an emergency system. In any emergency, call 911 (or your local emergency number) directly.

Who this policy is for

  • Caregivers create the account and enter information from their phone.
  • The older adult (“the resident”) uses the tablet. They do not create an account; the caregiver sets everything up with a one-time code.

By using Compasse, the caregiver confirms they are authorized to provide the resident’s information and that the resident is aware the assistant listens and records when spoken to.

What we collect

You provide:

  • Account details — caregiver name, email, and phone number.
  • Resident profile — the name you choose for them and their time zone.
  • Care content — medications, appointments/events, reminders, reassurance messages, and the short answers/phrases caregivers record.
  • Voice recordings — (a) what the resident says to the assistant, so it can understand and respond, and (b) voice messages and phrases caregivers record.

Collected automatically:

  • Device & usage data — push-notification token, app version, basic device status (e.g. a “heartbeat” so caregivers know the tablet is online), and diagnostic logs.
  • Crash reports — if a crash occurs, technical error data to help us fix it.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and we do not use the information for advertising or sell it.

How we use it

  • Run the service: show the board, deliver reminders and messages, answer the resident in a calm voice, place caregiver calls, and notify the caregiver of key events (a missed medication, a check-in answer, a call request, and a “tablet offline” notice).
  • Keep the app working and improve it during the trial (diagnostics, fixing bugs).

Only your family’s account and the Compasse team can access your family’s information.

How it’s protected

Information is stored with our service provider (Supabase) with access controls (row-level security) so one family cannot access another’s data, and is encrypted in transit. Service credentials are kept server-side and are never shipped in the app.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active and for the duration of the trial. You can ask us to delete it at any time (below), and we delete trial data when a family leaves the program.

Your choices and rights

  • Stop anytime — you can stop using Compasse for any reason.
  • Access or delete — email Aidan@revisesolutions.com to request a copy or deletion of your family’s information, including voice recordings. We will confirm when deletion is complete.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (to access, correct, or delete personal information). Contact us and we’ll honor them.

Children

Compasse is intended for adults. It is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Recording notice

The assistant records audio only when the resident speaks to it (e.g. taps to talk). Caregivers choose what phrases and messages to record. The caregiver confirms the resident is aware of, and consents to, this recording for the purpose of using Compasse.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Compasse evolves. We’ll post the updated version here and revise the “Last updated” date above.
