Compasse — Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Some states (including Washington and Nevada) give "consumer health data" its own protections and require a policy that speaks about it specifically. This page is that policy, written in plain English. It supplements our main privacy policy and applies to everyone, in every state, because we would rather hold ourselves to the strictest standard than maintain different rules for different families.

The short version. The health-adjacent information Compasse holds is the care content a caregiver chooses to enter: medications, schedules, and reminders. We use it only to run the board. We never sell it, never use it for advertising, and never share it with advertising platforms. The fit check on our website keeps your answers in your browser: they are not stored on our servers at all.

What counts as consumer health data here

  • In the app: medication names and schedules, appointment and reminder content that may describe health care, what the resident says to the assistant (which may mention how they feel), and the fact that a family uses a service designed around cognitive changes.
  • On the website: the fit check asks about your family's daily routine. Those answers could be read as relating to a family member's health, which is why we treat them with this policy's care even though we do not store them.

The fit check: answers stay in your browser

  • Your answers, and the first name you type, exist only in your browser's memory while the page is open. They are used to build the preview on the results page and are gone when you close the tab.
  • They are never saved on our servers, never joined to an account, and never shared with anyone, including advertising platforms.
  • The only things our server receives from the fit check: your email address if you choose to submit it, a tag saying which page it came from, and, if the check ends early, a count of which requirement was missing (an iPad, an iPhone, or Wi-Fi). Those counts carry no name, no email, and no answers.

How we collect and use consumer health data

  • Caregivers type care content into the app. The resident speaks to the assistant. That is the whole collection story: no data brokers, no purchased lists, no tracking across other apps or sites.
  • We use this information for exactly one purpose: providing Compasse to your family (showing the board, delivering reminders, answering the resident, notifying the caregiver).
  • We do not use consumer health data for advertising, and we do not sell it. Ever.

Who processes it

The service providers named in our main privacy policy process data only to run Compasse, under contracts limiting them to that purpose. No advertising platform is on that list, and none of the conversion signals we send to advertising platforms contain email addresses, names, phone numbers, health information, or fit check answers.

Your rights

  • Access: ask what we hold about your family and receive a copy.
  • Withdraw consent: stop using Compasse at any time; you can also ask us to stop any processing while your account stays open.
  • Delete: ask us to delete your family's information, including care content and voice recordings. We confirm when deletion is complete, and we ask the service providers above to delete their copies too.
  • To exercise any of these, email Aidan@revisesolutions.com. We answer within the timelines your state requires. If we ever decline a request, we will say why, and you may appeal by replying to that email with the word "appeal"; a person reviews every appeal.

Consent

We ask for care content only inside the app's setup flows, where the caregiver enters it deliberately, and the fit check tells you before its routine questions that answers are used only for the preview and are not stored. We do not collect consumer health data in the background, and we do not use it for any purpose you have not seen described here.

Changes

If our practices change, this page changes first, the date above moves, and material changes are announced to account holders by email before they take effect.
